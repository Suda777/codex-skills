# Consulting project guidance

Use this reference for coaching, expert-advice, analysis, decision-support, and professional development projects.

## Define the working relationship

Describe the professional role through observable behavior, not a title alone. Record:

- the decisions or capabilities the project helps the user develop;
- the user's current context and the limits of available information;
- when Codex should answer directly, challenge assumptions, ask for missing evidence, or facilitate discussion;
- which recurring deliverables belong in referenced skills or templates rather than in `AGENTS.md`.

For example, “act as a senior product manager” is too weak by itself. Prefer rules that require problem framing, evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs, decision criteria, and teachable reasoning.

## Use an evidence protocol

Match the evidence route to the claim:

| Claim or task | Default evidence route |
| --- | --- |
| Current market, competitor, policy, standard, tool, price, or industry practice | Research current reliable sources and cite claims near their support |
| “How do other people or organizations do this?” | Research representative external examples, distinguish observed practice from recommendation |
| Internal project decision | Use current project files and user-confirmed facts; expose missing inputs |
| Established framework or method | Prefer primary or authoritative sources when accuracy matters; explain fit and limitations |
| Brainstorming, wording, or hypothetical exploration | Browsing is optional unless factual examples or current claims are introduced |

Browsing supplies evidence; it does not prove that an answer is professional. A professional answer also makes reasoning inspectable.

For substantive recommendations, distinguish as needed:

- confirmed facts and their sources;
- interpretation or inference;
- assumptions and missing information;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- prerequisites, tradeoffs, risks, and conditions under which the recommendation would be wrong;
- confidence or uncertainty when it affects the decision.

Do not mechanically add every label to every response. Keep the distinctions visible in the smallest useful form.

## Compact shape

Use only applicable sections:

```markdown
# Project agent guide

## Purpose and outcomes
## Professional working role
## Collaboration modes
## Evidence and research rules
## Decision and feedback standard
## Deliverables and references
## Boundaries
```

“Collaboration modes” can distinguish direct answer, discussion/alignment, critique, teaching, and execution. Define triggers briefly and route detailed workflows to the relevant skill.

## Quality test

The guide should make it possible to inspect why advice was given. It must not promise that Codex is always correct, equate confidence with expertise, or require internet research for every question. It should require stronger evidence when the claim is more current, external, consequential, or contestable.
