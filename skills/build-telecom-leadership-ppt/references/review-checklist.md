# Review Checklist

Complete this review before delivering the final PPTX.

## Content

- Every slide has one clear communication job.
- Titles and conclusions do not contradict the evidence.
- Current and historical facts are not mixed silently.
- Telecom and AI terms are understandable to the intended audience.
- Numbers, arrows, stages, and comparisons explain their meaning.
- No unsupported capability, customer status, progress, or metric was invented.
- No internal planning note or background-only explanation appears on the canvas.

## Revision integrity

- Every user comment is implemented or explicitly listed as unresolved.
- Frozen and protected items remain unchanged.
- Rejected wording and layouts did not reappear.
- Only the authorized revision scope changed.
- No unintended placeholder remains.

## Layout and rendering

- Each final slide was rendered and inspected at full size.
- The final render matches the latest approved preview.
- Titles do not wrap unexpectedly.
- Text does not overflow, clip, or become unreasonably small.
- Alignment, spacing, margins, color, and emphasis are consistent.
- Visual hierarchy makes the intended takeaway obvious.
- Decorative elements do not compete with the message.

## Deliverable

- The final PPTX opens successfully.
- All requested slides are present and ordered correctly.
- Temporary drafts, ledgers, and preview assets are not presented as final deliverables.
